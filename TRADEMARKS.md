# FortressOS — project identity and unofficial distributions

The original **FortressOS** project was created by **GlueOfTrue** (https://github.com/GlueOfTrue). The canonical official source and releases are intended to be published through the repository controlled by the project maintainers, under the name **FortressOS-Official**. Until that repository is actually created and made public, do not treat a similarly named account or repository as official.

## Source code versus identity

The first-party source code is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The license allows study, modification, redistribution, sublicensing as applicable, and independent builds subject to its terms.

That license does not grant permission to suggest that an independently modified product is an **official FortressOS release**, is maintained by GlueOfTrue, or has been endorsed, reviewed, signed or secured by the upstream project.

This document is **not an additional condition on the source-code license**. It describes branding expectations and the distinction between upstream and downstream. No registered-trademark status is asserted; the scope and enforceability of any brand or passing-off claims depend on actual rights and applicable law.

## Permitted descriptive attribution

Accurate descriptions are welcome, including:

- "Based on the open-source FortressOS project, originally created by GlueOfTrue."
- "An independent fork of FortressOS."
- "Includes FortressOS components licensed under Apache-2.0."

Preserve required license, copyright and NOTICE information. Apache-2.0 Section 4 requires modified source files to carry prominent notices of modification.

## Independent releases

Downstream distributors should clearly identify the person or entity responsible for their fork and support. They should distinguish the product name, download location, binaries, signatures, release notes and branding from the official upstream wherever confusion might arise.

For example:

> ProjectName — an independently modified derivative of FortressOS. This build is not developed, reviewed, signed or supported by the official FortressOS maintainers. The downstream distributor is responsible for its own modifications, testing, updates and representations.

No upstream warranty or security certification applies to third-party changes. The Apache-2.0 license provides the actual legal warranty and liability terms, subject to applicable law; this guidance does not impose a new blanket indemnity or claim absolute immunity.

## Reporting confusion or requesting brand permission

Open a discussion or issue in the official project repository for a brand-use question. Source contributions are welcome through pull requests, but no fork is legally required to submit changes upstream.
